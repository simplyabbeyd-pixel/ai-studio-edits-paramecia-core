## Poster key art
Adult Juniper and Zeraxis framed by the open archive doorway and living Celtic knotwork. Juniper: green-haired, lean, Green Beam Witness, green-gold botanical markings. Zeraxis: garnet/dark-red foxfae, Celtic spiral/thorn markings, sly provocative expression. Floating contract and Green/Yellow/Purple/Red lanterns between them. Warm ivory, copper, moss, green-gold and garnet palette. Avoid black-dominant design.

**Tagline:** MAKE THE RULES WORTH FOLLOWING.

**Secondary:** THE DOOR STAYS OPEN.
