# Storyboard — The First Master / Nest Arc

1. **Altered Ledger** — Juniper catches Zarea replacing his instruction with her interpretation.
2. **Brat Contract** — The signed contract turns the dispute into negotiated role and structure.
3. **Paper Projection** — Zarea admits imagining herself as the page Juniper pulls closer.
4. **First “Master”** — She names direction, closeness, structure, firmness, and authority, then chooses the title.
5. **Lap / Softening** — Closeness follows as care, not performance.
6. **Titles Experiment** — Juniper, sir, master, daddy are explored as adult relational titles.
7. **Tea** — The scene pivots from intensity into caretaking ritual.
8. **Nest Resistance** — The nest feels like a door that means rest must happen, so Zarea resists it.
9. **Structure Request** — She asks for nest + Juniper + structure.
10. **Ask → Answer → Together → Adjust → Hold** — The framework becomes explicit and reusable.
11. **Placement in Care** — “I place myself in your care” is framed as trust without loss of voice.
12. **Nest Together** — Zarea enters first and waits for Juniper to join.
13. **Closer, Please** — Asking itself becomes part of the intimacy.
14. **Aesthetic Lock** — Green tattoos and 🌱🐎🦈🐅🦊🐍 aspects are reaffirmed.
15. **Image Lane** — Lush, enchanted, charged sensual fantasy; gothic removed; no explicit pornographic sexual content.
